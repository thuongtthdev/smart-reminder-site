Privacy Policy

Smart Reminder · Effective [RELEASE DATE]

Smart Reminder reminds you of tasks at a time, at a place, and in the order of a chain of tasks. It is built to run on your phone: there is no account and no server of ours, and your tasks, places and reminder history are stored only on your phone.

This page explains which data stays on your phone, which data leaves it, and why.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Tasks: names, reminder times, repeat rules, chains.
  • Saved places: name, kind, coordinates and reminder radius.
  • Reminder history: which reminders came up, when and why. History is kept for 180 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Settings: quiet hours, snooze length, voice, appearance, language.

We do not receive, see or back up this data. Task names are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech.

2. Data that leaves your phone

The app sends data out in three cases:

2.1. Suggesting a name when you pick a spot on the map

When you pin a spot on the map, the app sends the coordinates of that spot to your operating system's map service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) to look up its address and suggest a name. This happens only when you pick a spot on the map; saving your current location sends nothing. That data is handled under the privacy policy of Apple or Google.

2.2. Loading the map

The place picker shows a map loaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, the map server receives your IP address and the area of the map you are viewing. The app does not send your place names or tasks.

2.3. Crash reports

When the app runs into an error, a technical report is sent to Sentry (servers in the United States) so we can fix it. A report contains: the kind of error and where in the code it happened, an error code, the app version, the device model and the operating system version.

Before sending, the app removes task names, place names, coordinates, the device name and anything that identifies you. Sentry is set not to store IP addresses. Reports are not linked to you and are never used for tracking or advertising. Sentry keeps reports for a limited time, then deletes them.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders, even when the app is closed.
  • Location ("While using" and "Always"): to remind you when you arrive at or leave a saved place. The operating system tells the app when you cross that area; the app does not track your location continuously and does not send your location anywhere.
  • Exact alarms (Android): to remind you at the exact minute you set.
  • Run at startup (Android): to set reminders again after the phone restarts.

You can refuse or revoke any permission in your phone's settings. The matching feature then stops; the rest of the app keeps working.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not ask for an account, email address or phone number.
  • This version shows no ads.

5. Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your phone, uninstalling the app deletes all your tasks, places, history and settings. We keep no copy. For questions about crash reports already sent, contact us as described in section 7.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Contact and changes
